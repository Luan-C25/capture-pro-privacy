Capture Pro - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 27, 2026

Overview

Capture Pro ("the Extension") is a screenshot tool that captures and saves images from websites. We take your privacy seriously and are committed to transparency about how we handle your data.

What We Collect

What we DO NOT collect:

We do not collect any personally identifiable information (name, email, address, etc.)
We do not track your browsing history
We do not collect analytics or usage data
We do not monitor your activity
We do not use cookies or tracking pixels
We do not sell or share any user data

What is stored locally:

Screenshots you capture are stored only on your device in your Downloads folder
Extension settings (email, license key, preferences) are stored locally using Chrome's storage API
This data never leaves your device
How Your Data is Used

All data is used exclusively for the following purposes:

Storing captured screenshots on your device
Maintaining your extension settings and preferences
Tracking daily capture limits (for free tier)
Data Storage
All screenshots and settings are stored locally on your device
No data is transmitted to external servers
No data is backed up to cloud services
When you uninstall the Extension, all locally stored data is deleted
Third Parties

We do not share data with third parties. The Extension does not connect to external services except for:

Chrome Web Store (for updates)
Payment processors (only if you purchase a Pro license)
Your Rights

You have the right to:

View, modify, or delete your locally stored data at any time
Uninstall the Extension and have all data deleted
Contact us with privacy concerns
Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the updated policy on this page.

Summary

Capture Pro is privacy-first. We don't collect, track, or share any of your data. Everything stays on your device. That's it.
